01 / WHO I AM

Building interfaces
between light and sound.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of California, Berkeley, working with Prof. Mengjie Yu. Before Berkeley, I spent six years at The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

My research spans the design, fabrication, and characterization of high-performance micro- and nanodevices across lithium niobate, silicon, and silica photonic platforms.

02 / WHAT I EXPLORE

PHOTONICS

Etchless integrated photonics

Using bound states in the continuum to confine and guide light with low-index waveguides—without etching the single-crystal substrate.

MECHANICS

Mechanical BICs

Engineering robust optomechanical resonators that suppress mechanical dissipation and remain tolerant to fabrication variations.

TRANSDUCTION

Light ↔ sound

Leveraging the piezoelectric and nonlinear properties of lithium niobate for acousto-optic modulation, microwave photonics, and quantum interfaces.
